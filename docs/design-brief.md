# Surface brief — Communication PhD Program Explorer (all routes)

Scope: the whole site (Match, Programs, Faculty, Shortlist, About). Visitor mode: **Operate** (search, filter,
compare) with the Match page doubling as the product's own demonstration. Platform: web, responsive.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a research-fit instrument, not a brochure. It refuses the category default (cream paper,
serif display, one warm accent, same-size cards) and the original site's lime/forest look. One loud signature — the
broadcast test-card colour bars — carries identity; everything else is quiet, dense and legible.

OWN-WORLD: Ground: light "studio white" (#F6F6F3) with graphite ink (#16181D); dark: deep slate (#14161A) with
chalk (#E9EAE6). One action colour: signal blue (#1F6FEB light / #5B9CFF dark). Five lens colours borrowed from the
SMPTE bars, tuned for contrast: topic yellow (#E0B100), approach cyan (#00A3B4), data green (#2E9E5B), setting
magenta (#C9399E), region blue (#3B6FE0). Signature components: the six-bar wordmark; the "signal strength" score
glyph (five bars) next to every match number; chips tinted per lens; the model-download bar that lights up bar by
bar. Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display, width axis used for the hero), Schibsted Grotesk (UI and body),
tabular numerals everywhere numbers align. Radii 10px controls / 14px panels; elevation = one 1px border OR one
offset soft shadow, never both.

STORY: "Describe your research; see who could advise you and where; keep a list." The visitor understands that
matching is local and private, that labels are interpretive, and that programs — not only people — are the unit
of decision.

FIRST VIEWPORT (Match page, desktop ≥1024px): header with wordmark left, nav (Match · Programs · Faculty ·
Shortlist · About), theme + language toggles right. Below: two columns 5/7. Left: the headline in Bricolage
("Find the Communication faculty and programs that fit your research"), one sentence of help, the textarea
(8 rows, placeholder is a real example), suggested-filter chips (appear after typing), the primary button
"Find matches". Right: before the first search, a compact explainer of how matching works (three short lines, no
icons) and the two counts (programs, faculty) set as text, not hero metrics. After a search the right column becomes
the results stream with a Faculty / Programs segmented switch at the top. On mobile the columns stack.

FORM: Operate directory with a sticky input rail; position 1 of my ordered list (others: full-screen map-first,
spreadsheet grid, conversational chat, card wall). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md,
and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
